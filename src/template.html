Going to…

Timetables and fares from the Transport Department's open data (updated __BUILT__) and Star Ferry's published frequencies. Times are 24-hour, Hong Kong time. Sailings after midnight appear at the end of the list.

Ferries can be cancelled or changed for typhoons, black rainstorm warnings and special events. Check the operator before a tight connection. On a public holiday, tap Sun / holiday at the top.

† on a time means the operator attaches a footnote (for example a different vessel or a stop). It isn't spelled out in the open data.
